# Concepts of Information Society Coursework

A curated collection of Macedonian-language Bachelor coursework completed for the **Concepts of Information Society** course at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje.

The collection focuses on digital-payment concepts, mobile-service design, online knowledge exchange, and the social and technological changes associated with contemporary information systems.

## Contents

### 1. DotCard group project

`projects/dotcard-group-project-mk.pdf`

A group concept for a mobile payment ecosystem intended to expand the use of payment cards and reward users through referral points and gift-card benefits. The report discusses perceived limitations of existing payment practices, proposes product features, references React Native and Firebase as possible implementation technologies, and presents interface mockups for the imagined application.

The project is a **conceptual design and research artifact**. The archive does not contain application source code or evidence of a deployed prototype.

### 2. UniCard individual project

`projects/unicard-individual-project-mk.pdf`

An individual extension of the DotCard idea by **Ariton Verush**. UniCard explores cardless ATM withdrawals through a mobile application linked to a user's banking account and protected through fingerprint authentication. The report also discusses transaction history, payment-card management, reward points, gift-card benefits, and possible cryptocurrency-based rewards.

The document includes product-flow descriptions and interface mockups. It is not a production banking design, implemented biometric system, or validated security architecture.

### 3. Quora knowledge-exchange laboratory proposal

`proposed-laboratory/quora-knowledge-exchange-lab-proposal-mk.pdf`

A proposed laboratory exercise centered on a cross-platform knowledge-sharing service. The exercise asks students to explore user profiles, spaces, questions, answers, and topic-based discovery as examples of online knowledge exchange within an information society.

## Public portfolio curation

The original archive also contained routine laboratory submissions involving Google services, LinkedIn, Freelancer, and Trello. Those files were excluded because they primarily consisted of account-setup screenshots, exposed personal or contact information, depended heavily on outdated service interfaces, and added limited long-term portfolio value.

For this public edition:

- student identification numbers and academic email addresses were removed;
- trailing blank pages were removed from the project reports;
- editable Office files were converted to PDF where appropriate;
- document metadata and filenames were normalized;
- the original Macedonian academic content and visible author attribution were preserved.

## Limitations

These materials represent early Bachelor coursework and should be interpreted in that context. The payment applications are product concepts rather than implemented or security-audited financial systems. Some technology assumptions and platform references reflect the period in which the coursework was written and may now be outdated. No user study, threat model, banking integration, biometric validation, or production deployment is included.
