# Notice

## Scope

This repository is an archival portfolio version of laboratory coursework from the Object-Oriented Analysis and Design course. It contains six student-created UML submissions for a shared online-market requirements scenario.

## Excluded Material

The original archive also contained six instructor-provided laboratory briefs. They were excluded because they repeat the same detailed requirements text, are not the student's authored work, and are unnecessary for understanding the preserved diagrams.

The original archive and student-number-based filenames were also excluded.

## Preserved Content

The diagram content and Macedonian labels are preserved as submitted. Files were renamed and organized, but the underlying models were not redesigned or presented as production-ready architecture.

## Limitations

- The collection contains analysis and design models only; it does not include application source code or a running system.
- Some diagrams are dense and reflect the conventions and tooling used at the time of the course.
- The models were created for educational assessment and were not formally validated against an implemented system.
- The diagrams may contain minor notation, naming, or modeling inconsistencies typical of introductory laboratory work.
