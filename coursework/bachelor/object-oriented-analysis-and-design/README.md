# Object-Oriented Analysis and Design - UML Laboratories

A curated collection of six individual laboratory exercises completed for the **Object-Oriented Analysis and Design** course during the Bachelor's degree.

The laboratories model a shared **online market management system** and demonstrate a progression through core object-oriented analysis and UML techniques. The original coursework and diagram labels are in Macedonian.

## Coursework Scope

The modeled system supports supermarkets and prepared-food markets, product and speciality discovery, shopping carts and orders, customer payments, employee workflows, delivery preparation, discount cards, receipts, and a distributed deployment across central and replicated servers.

## Laboratory Diagrams

| Laboratory | UML artifact | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use-case diagram | System actors, customer ordering, employee operations, payment, and delivery responsibilities |
| 2 | Activity diagrams | Customer and employee workflows, branching, parallel activities, and process completion |
| 3 | Class diagram | Domain entities, attributes, operations, inheritance, associations, aggregation, and composition |
| 4 | State-machine diagrams | Order, payment, product, speciality, and employee-state transitions |
| 5 | Sequence and collaboration diagrams | Time-ordered interactions and object communication during ordering and payment scenarios |
| 6 | Deployment diagram | Central server, replicated database copy, market workstations, mobile/web clients, and printers |

## Opening the Editable Diagrams

The `.drawio` files can be opened in [diagrams.net](https://app.diagrams.net/) or a compatible editor. The PNG files preserve the submitted visual output without requiring special software.
