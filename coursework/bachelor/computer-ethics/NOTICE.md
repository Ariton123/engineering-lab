This folder is a cleaned public portfolio version of coursework originally completed for the Computer Ethics course.

- removed the student identification number from visible content and filenames
- converted the two editable Word reports into PDF format
- converted the PowerPoint presentation into PDF format

- removed document metadata that was not needed for public distribution
- excluded the original ZIP and editable Office source files

## Academic and Methodological Limitations

The materials represent Bachelor-level coursework from 2021-2022 and should not be treated as current legal, policy, medical, military, or cybersecurity guidance.

The news journal and reports rely largely on contemporary online news, public articles, and secondary sources. Some links may become unavailable or their content may change over time.

The DARPA report is mainly a descriptive overview of the Measuring Biological Aptitude program. Ethical implications such as consent, biological-data privacy, surveillance, discrimination, and automated decision-making are relevant to the subject but were not developed as a full formal ethics framework in the original assignment.

The brief presentation contains only a high-level summary and is included for academic completeness.

The original wording and conclusions were preserved except for privacy cleanup and format conversion.
