# Computer Ethics Coursework

## Overview

This folder preserves individual coursework completed for the **Computer Ethics** course during the Bachelor's degree.

The material examines ethical and professional questions connected to software systems, privacy, cybersecurity, trust, access, ownership, social impact, artificial intelligence, critical-system failures, and emerging biological-data technologies.

The coursework is written in **Macedonian** and was completed during the 2021-2022 academic period.

## Included Materials

### Computer Ethics News Journal

`computer-ethics-news-journal-mk.pdf`

A semester journal summarizing and reflecting on selected technology-related news across the following ethical areas:

- ethics and artificial intelligence
- privacy
- trust
- cybersecurity and safety
- access
- intellectual property and ownership
- societal influence
- professional conduct

The journal also identifies five news stories considered especially important and explains their ethical relevance.

### Software Failure and Professional Conduct Report

`reports/software-failure-professional-conduct-report-mk.pdf`

An individual forum-based report examining the consequences of software failure and unprofessional engineering behavior. Discussed examples include:

- a privacy failure involving Eufy security cameras
- the Boeing 737 MAX and safety-critical software responsibility
- a sentencing-calculation software defect that released prisoners earlier than intended

The report connects these incidents to professional accountability, privacy, reliability, and safety-critical systems.

### DARPA Measuring Biological Aptitude Report

`reports/darpa-measuring-biological-aptitude-report-mk.pdf`

A research assignment describing DARPA's Measuring Biological Aptitude program and its proposed use of biomarkers, phenotype information, cognitive measurements, and physiological data to evaluate and improve military readiness.

The original report is primarily descriptive, but the topic also raises broader computer-ethics questions involving consent, privacy, biological-data governance, surveillance, fairness, and automated personnel assessment.

### DARPA Presentation

`presentation/darpa-measuring-biological-aptitude-presentation.pdf`

A brief presentation accompanying the DARPA research assignment.
