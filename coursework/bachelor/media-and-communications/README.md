# Media and Communications - Online Courses and Educational Videos

## Overview

This folder preserves coursework completed for **Медиуми и комуникации (Media and Communications)** during the sixth semester of the Bachelor's degree at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering (FINKI), Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje.

The work explored online education as a communication medium and combined a group research project with short educational videos for younger learners. The original coursework materials are written in Macedonian.

## Final Project: Online Courses

The final project investigates awareness and use of online courses among students and school pupils in North Macedonia.

The report includes:

- motivation and project objectives
- hypotheses concerning online learning
- an online survey with 154 responses
- descriptive comparisons across participant age groups
- an activity and communication plan
- educational video production
- discussion of the project's intended social and educational impact
- documented responsibilities of each team member

The project concluded that online courses were more familiar and more frequently used among university-age respondents than among younger participants, while also identifying opportunities to promote accessible online educational content.
