# Balanced Workforce — Design of Governance in Socio-Technical Information Systems

## Overview

Balanced Workforce is a socio-technical systems design project developed for the **Design of Governance in Socio-Technical Information Systems** course at the University of Neuchâtel.

The project explores a platform-based model for temporary inter-company employee leasing. The central idea is to help companies balance workforce demand and availability by allowing companies with underutilized employees to temporarily share skilled workers with companies facing staffing gaps, project-specific needs, or short-term workload increases.

## Project Context

Many companies struggle to adapt their workforce quickly to changing market or project demands. Recruiting and training new employees can be expensive and slow, while other companies may simultaneously have skilled employees without active project assignments.

Balanced Workforce proposes a governed digital platform that supports temporary workforce exchange between companies while considering:

- legal compliance
- employee consent and autonomy
- data privacy
- secure communication
- contractual traceability
- business model sustainability
- integration with existing HR and ERP environments

## System Concept

The proposed system includes:

- **Company 1 / Lessor**: the company that has temporarily available employees
- **Company 2 / Lessee**: the company that needs temporary skilled workforce
- **Employees**: workers who voluntarily participate in leasing opportunities
- **Platform**: a secure intermediary for matching, transaction tracking, and coordination
- **Connectors**: certified integration components for ERP, HR, or standalone systems
- **Database / Logging**: infrastructure for traceability, auditability, and non-personal transactional data
- **Contract Management**: support for negotiation, agreement creation, signing, and storage

## Governance Focus

The project studies not only the technical architecture, but also the governance mechanisms required to make such a system viable.

Important governance dimensions include:

- labor law compliance
- data protection and privacy
- contractual agreements
- employee consent
- intellectual property rights
- conflict of interest management
- transparency and fair treatment
- platform security and scalability
- dispute resolution
